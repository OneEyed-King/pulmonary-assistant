# PulmoLens — 2-Minute Demo Script

Rough pacing guide — read naturally, don't rush. Adjust wording to your own voice.

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

"Pulmonologists lose a lot of time just clicking around before they even start talking to a
patient — vitals in one tab, labs in another, last visit's notes somewhere else. I built
PulmoLens to collapse all of that into one view, purpose-built for pulmonology, on top of a real
FHIR patient record — not a mockup."

## 0:20–0:55 — The core feature: one comprehensive view

*(Open a patient's chart, scroll the Overview tab.)*

"This is the core of PulmoLens: everything a pulmonologist needs before and during a consult, on
a single screen. Demographics, active conditions and medications, allergies, vitals, lab and PFT
trends versus the last visit, and structured notes from previous appointments — no tab-hopping,
no hunting across systems. For pre-consult prep, a doctor can scan a patient's entire recent
history in seconds instead of clicking through five different screens."

*(Point out the AI Physician Brief and Clinical Changes list.)*

"On top of that view, an AI-generated brief and an automatic clinical-changes check flag what's
actually different since last time, so nothing important gets buried in the scroll."

## 0:55–1:15 — Proactive, not just reactive

*(Back to homepage — Care Gaps panel.)*

"And it's not just single-patient view — the homepage's Care Gaps panel scans every patient and
flags anyone overdue for follow-up, or missed after an ER visit, before the doctor even opens a
chart."

## 1:15–1:50 — Visit workflow: meds, notes, finalize

*(Click Start Visit.)*

"Opening a patient to see them starts the visit. The doctor stages medications from a curated
list, then writes quick shorthand that AI Note Assist expands into a structured SOAP note —
grounded only in what was actually written, never inventing a finding. Everything — medications,
notes, the encounter itself — is reviewed on one final screen and only commits to the FHIR
record when the doctor confirms."

## 1:50–2:00 — Close

"One view for everything a pulmonologist needs, and a workflow that gets out of the way the rest
of the time. That's PulmoLens — thanks for watching."
